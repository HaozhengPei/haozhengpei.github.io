ICLR 2027 · Under reviewMay 2026–present

Certified Mode-Recall for
Generative Models

First author

A finite-sample framework for certifying whether a generative model covers a mode, even when the classifier used to label samples is imperfect. The framework returns present, absent, or inconclusive conclusions conditional on the labeller.

I developed validity and consistency results, with sample-complexity bounds for rare-mode certification, and evaluated GANs, flow matching, and pretrained diffusion models on StackedMNIST, CIFAR-10, and CelebA subgroups.

Generative model evaluation · Finite-sample bounds · Diffusion & flow matching
Methods & experiments +

Experiments include 1,000-mode StackedMNIST and 32 CelebA subgroups, using up to 40,000 generated samples per experiment. The study examines mode coverage alongside conventional generative-model metrics, including cases where nearest-neighbor recall and FID miss losses in subgroup coverage.

Research Assistant · Prof. Bo Zhu’s group

The project explores constructing simulation environments from real-world images and videos, then generating robot tasks, demonstrations, and training data for vision-language-action models.

Clink! Chop! Thud!
Learning Object Sounds from Real-World Interactions

Third author · Georgia Tech

Learning which object in a scene produces an interaction sound from in-the-wild egocentric video. The project connects audio and visual representations to localize sounding objects.

I contributed annotation and feature pipelines for object-level supervision, and ran evaluations and ablations on EPIC-KITCHENS and Ego4D.

Probabilistic Forecasting for
Wind Generation and Power Markets

Probabilistic wind and solar forecasts that connect uncertainty in renewable generation to imbalance pricing, dispatch, and price formation.

I worked with domain-informed Transformer and DKFormer models, ARIMA and SVM baselines, and spatiotemporal features from turbine and weather data covering more than 10,000 assets. The work used ensemble learning and Bayesian model combination to combine forecasts.
